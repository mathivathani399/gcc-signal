Provider evidence · no paid ranking

Provider evidence

Build a comparable evidence file before selecting a setup advisor, operator, recruiter or real estate partner. Public capability claims are not the same as independently verified outcomes.

No ratings published
Neutrality rule: providers may sponsor content or claim profiles, but payment cannot change their evidence score or rank. A score is withheld until reference checks and comparable completed-project evidence exist.

Evidence policy

How the product avoids becoming another biased directory

Separate the claim from the proof. Use first-party claims as discovery evidence only.

Show the denominator. If provider outcomes are later scored, publish sample size, recency and inclusion rules.

Let records be corrected. Providers can submit corrections, but source-backed review controls publication.

No pay-to-rank. Commercial placement is clearly labelled and isolated from evidence scores.
